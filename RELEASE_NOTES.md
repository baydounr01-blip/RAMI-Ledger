- **Mapa 3D de Tenerife** hecho con datos abiertos (relieve real a ~67 m por píxel, costa, Teide, pueblos, aeropuertos y carreteras principales), incluido en la app: no hace falta internet para verlo.

- **Gafas de realidad virtual:** botón «🥽 Gafas VR» (WebXR) para Quest, Pico o SteamVR con un navegador compatible.
- **Panel en cinco idiomas** (v0.5.5) e instalación/actualización sin terminal (v0.5.4).
- **Marco legal, claro:** no se usa Google Maps ni Google Earth (sus condiciones prohíben extraer sus datos). RAMI-Chain no verifica que una empresa exista ni sea legal: el panel enlaza al Registro Mercantil y al BORME para que lo compruebes tú. Sin cambios de consenso (no hay hard fork respecto a v0.5.x). Testnet experimental, sin valor monetario.

## What's new in v0.6.0 — Tenerife in 3D: the metaverse on the real island

- **3D map of Tenerife** built from open data (real relief at ~67 m per pixel, coastline, the Teide, towns, airports and main roads), bundled in the app: no internet needed to see it.
- **Every parcel is a real place:** the 32×32 RAMI City grid covers the whole island (2.6 km cells). "Locate my company" looks an address up on OpenStreetMap (only when you press the button) and takes you to its parcel.
- **Fast graphics:** WebGL with everything instanced, terrain level of detail, smooth orbit camera, Island and City views, town labels. The dashboard no longer repaints anything if the city did not change. 2D fallback view.
- **VR headsets:** "🥽 VR headset" button (WebXR) for Quest, Pico or SteamVR with a WebXR browser.
- **Dashboard in five languages** (v0.5.5) and install/update without a terminal (v0.5.4).
- **Legal framing, stated plainly:** no Google Maps or Google Earth data is used (their terms forbid extracting it). RAMI-Chain does not verify that a company exists or is legal: the dashboard links to the Commercial Registry and the BORME so you can check. No consensus changes (no hard fork versus v0.5.x). Experimental testnet, no monetary value.
